## [1.0.0] - 2024-05-24

First version, as delivered for the school assignment.

### Added

- Notebooks analysing a call-center dataset: first call (`FirstCall`), second call (`SecondCall`), call length (`Call_Length`), change of caller (`Change_Caller`) and data preparation (`Initiation`).
- Bayesian networks built with pyAgrum to estimate answer probabilities by weekday and hour.
- Heatmaps of answer probability and call duration by weekday and hour.
